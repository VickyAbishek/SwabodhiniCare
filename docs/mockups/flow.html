Who does what

Five roles. One person can hold more than one (for example, a Centre Head who also fills in applications). Nobody can approve an application they filled in themselves.

ADAdminநிர்வாகி
Does
Sets up staff accounts, resets forgotten passwords, checks backups and storage.
Enters
Staff name, email, mobile, role(s), centre.
Does not
Approve applications (unless they also hold an approver role).
THTherapistசிகிச்சையாளர்
Does
Meets the parent, fills in the 11-step application, takes the photo and documents, gets the parent's signature, sends it on.
Enters
Everything about the applicant, plus their own observations and recommendation.
Also
Fixes and resends applications that were sent back.
TYTherapy Headசிகிச்சைத் தலைவர்
Checks
Clinical fit: is our program right for this person?
Enters
Approve, Send back or Reject, with a comment.
Also
Sees reports for all centres.
CHCentre Headமையத் தலைவர்
Checks
Capacity: is there a seat, a batch and staff at this centre?
Enters
Approve, Send back or Reject, with a comment.
Also
Sees reports; can fill in applications too.
DRDirectorஇயக்குநர்
Decides
Admit, Waitlist or Reject, then signs with their password.
Enters
Decision, optional comment, password. Their signature is uploaded once and stamped automatically.
Also
Can reopen a locked application, with a reason.

Other paths

Not every application goes straight through. These are the other ways a file can move.

Sent back, fixed, sent again

Waiting: Centre Headகாத்திருப்பு: மையத் தலைவர் → Send back + comment → Sent backதிருப்பி அனுப்பப்பட்டது → therapist fixes → Waiting: Therapy Headகாத்திருப்பு: சிகிச்சைத் தலைவர்

Any reviewer can send it back. It always starts again at the Therapy Head, so the clinical check sees the changes. Earlier comments stay on the routing slip. See B1.

Rejected

Any review stage → Reject + reason → Rejectedநிராகரிக்கப்பட்டது

Final. The reason is required and shown to the therapist, who tells the family in person. (No automatic messages to parents in the first version.)

Waitlisted, admitted later

Director → Waitlist → Waitlistedகாத்திருப்புப் பட்டியலில் → seat frees up → Director → Admit → Admittedசேர்க்கப்பட்டது

The waitlist report shows everyone waiting, by centre and program.

Withdrawn

Draftவரைவு or Sent backதிருப்பி அனுப்பப்பட்டது → family decides not to continue → Withdrawnதிரும்பப் பெறப்பட்டது

The therapist or Admin marks it withdrawn. Nothing is deleted straight away.

Reopened after signing

Admittedசேர்க்கப்பட்டது → Director or Admin reopens with a reason → Sent backதிருப்பி அனுப்பப்பட்டது

For corrections after signing. The old signature and fingerprint stay in the history.
